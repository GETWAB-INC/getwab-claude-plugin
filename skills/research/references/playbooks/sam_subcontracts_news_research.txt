Example 1
User:

    subAwardReportId AS subcontract_report_id,

Example 2
User:
Count subcontracts by state

Assistant:
Explanation:
This query counts subcontract records by subcontractor physical address state.

SQL:
SELECT
    entityPhysicalAddress__state__code AS state_code,
    count() AS subcontract_count
FROM sam_subcontracts.data
WHERE nullIf(trim(entityPhysicalAddress__state__code), '') IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY state_code
ORDER BY subcontract_count DESC
LIMIT 100

Example 3
User:
Top subcontractors by total subcontract amount

Assistant:
Explanation:
This query ranks subcontractors by the summed subcontract amount.

SQL:
SELECT
    subEntityLegalBusinessName AS subcontractor_name,
    subEntityUei AS subcontractor_uei,
    sum(toFloat64OrNull(subAwardAmount)) AS total_subaward_amount,
    count() AS subcontract_count
FROM sam_subcontracts.data
WHERE nullIf(trim(subEntityLegalBusinessName), '') IS NOT NULL
GROUP BY
    subcontractor_name,
    subcontractor_uei
ORDER BY total_subaward_amount DESC
LIMIT 50

MANDATORY SEMANTIC DISTINCTIONS

1. A row in `sam_subcontracts.data` is a reported contract subaward record.
2. `subAwardAmount` is the reported subcontract/subaward amount, not the prime
   contract ceiling, total obligations, opportunity value, or company revenue.
3. The prime entity and subcontractor are different roles. Never reverse them.
4. A historical subcontractor is not automatically an incumbent, bidder,
   teammate, preferred supplier, or likely winner.
5. Absence from this table is not evidence that a company has never
   subcontracted. Reporting scope and completeness may vary.
6. `submittedDate` is a reporting/submission timestamp; `subAwardDate` is the
   preferred business-event date for historical activity.
7. Arrays such as business types and prime NAICS may require ARRAY JOIN, but
   avoid row multiplication when summing money.

DIRECT AND BROAD MATCHING

Label evidence `direct_match` only when at least one strong identifier ties the
record to the subject:
- exact prime or subcontractor UEI;
- exact PIID/prime award identifier;
- exact subcontract report ID/UUID or subaward number;
- exact normalized legal business name together with another corroborating
  field.

Label evidence `broad_match` when based only on NAICS, agency, geography,
business type, keyword, or a name without a stable identifier. Always expose the
match basis in the result.

DEDUPLICATION AND MONEY

Before counting events or summing `subAwardAmount`, identify the record grain
using the strongest available report identifier. When multiple rows can
represent revisions or repeated ingestion, deduplicate using report ID/UUID,
subaward number, prime award identity, subcontractor identity, date, and amount
as appropriate. Return both raw row count and distinct-event count when
completeness matters. Never sum an array-expanded result without deduplicating
back to the original record.

RECOMMENDED NEWS AGGREGATIONS

- Recent reported subawards by `subAwardDate`.
- Top subcontractors by distinct reported subaward dollars and count.
- Top primes by reported subcontract dollars and distinct subcontractors.
- Agency × prime × subcontractor relationship summaries.
- NAICS concentration and year-over-year trend.
- Geographic distribution using subcontractor or place-of-performance fields;
  state which geography was selected.
- New entrant signal: first observed subaward date in this dataset.
- Recency signal: most recent observed subaward date.
- Concentration: top-1/top-5 share of reported dollars.
- Relationship persistence: distinct years or months with observed activity.
