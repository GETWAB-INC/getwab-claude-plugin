APPROVED SOURCE

Database: sba_subcontracting_plans
Table: sba_subcontracting_plans.contracts

Each row is a record from one official SBA fiscal-year workbook describing a
prime contractor/award or IDV associated with a subcontracting plan. The live
table combines all discovered annual files. This is not a table of individual
subcontract awards, open opportunities, or actual subcontractor payments.

DATA-GRAIN AND INTERPRETATION RULES

1. One row is one source-workbook row, not necessarily one globally unique
   contract across all years.
2. The same PIID or contractor may appear in multiple fiscal-year directories.
3. record_id identifies the source row; use normalized business identifiers and
   PIID for business-level deduplication.
4. The directory shows primes/awards with subcontracting plans. It does not show
   individual subcontractors, available subcontract work, bids, or subaward
   transactions.
5. total_contract_value is source-reported total contract value where available.
   It is not FPDS net obligations, current spending, subcontract dollars, amount
   available to small businesses, or company revenue.
6. fiscal_year is the source directory year, not necessarily award year.
7. performance_start_date, completion_date, and last_date_to_order have distinct
   meanings. Do not substitute one for another.
8. Empty UEI in older years may accompany DUNS-era records.
9. extra_fields_json is preservation metadata. Do not assume keys exist across
   years; inspect observed keys before using them.
10. loaded_at is ingestion freshness, not a business-event date.

IDENTITY AND DEDUPLICATION

Preferred prime identity:
1. unique_entity_id when non-empty;
2. duns_number for legacy records;
3. normalized legal_business_name plus corroborating location/parent.

Preferred award identity:
- PIID together with contracting agency/department when needed;
- IDV referenced PIID for parent vehicle analysis.

For cross-year unique awards, do not use count() alone. Use a documented key
such as tuple(piid, contracting_agency_name) after excluding empty PIIDs.
Return raw source rows and distinct business records separately when useful.

DATE AND STATUS RULES

- Potentially ongoing performance:
  performance_start_date <= today() AND
  (completion_date IS NULL OR completion_date >= today()).
- Ordering may remain open when last_date_to_order >= today().
- Do not call a record active solely because it appears in the latest workbook.
- Do not treat fiscal_year as signed date.
- Use explicit NULL handling for missing dates.

SEARCH RULES

- Prime contractor: UEI first, then DUNS, then normalized legal name.
- Parent: ultimate_parent_uei/DUNS/name.
- Award: piid.
- Vehicle: idv_referenced_piid.
- Buyer: department, agency, and office are separate levels.
- Market: naics_code and naics_description.
- Geography: vendor location and performance_state are different.
- Plan: subcontract_plan_type and IDV plan fields.
- Source provenance: fiscal_year, source_file, source_url, source_row_number.

DIRECT AND BROAD MATCHING

direct_match:
- exact UEI/DUNS;
- exact PIID or IDV referenced PIID;
- exact legal name plus corroborating agency/location/parent.

broad_match:
- NAICS;
- agency/department/office;
- state;
- plan type;
- name similarity without stable ID.

Always expose match level and fields used. A broad match is a research lead, not
proof that the record belongs to the same entity/award.

USEFUL AGGREGATIONS

- Prime contractors with subcontracting plans by agency/department/office.
- Distinct primes by NAICS, geography, plan type, and fiscal year.
- Cross-year persistence of a prime in SBA directories.
- Contracts/IDVs with future completion or ordering dates.
- Total source-reported contract value, with explicit non-obligation label.
- Parent-company concentration.
- New-to-directory and recurring-prime signals by UEI/DUNS.
- Agency × NAICS × prime relationship maps.

- Coverage and missing-field rates by fiscal year.

MONEY RULES

- Sum total_contract_value only after selecting the intended grain.
- For annual directory totals, group by fiscal_year and explain possible
  cross-year repetition.
- For cross-year business totals, deduplicate awards first.
- Never combine total_contract_value with FPDS obligations, SAM subcontract

- Prefer median, quantiles, count, and documented distinct counts alongside sums.

Safe language:

QUERY PATTERNS

Prime by UEI:
SELECT *
FROM sba_subcontracting_plans.contracts
WHERE unique_entity_id = 'UEI_VALUE'
ORDER BY fiscal_year DESC, piid ASC
LIMIT 100

Recurring primes:
SELECT
    unique_entity_id,
    any(legal_business_name) AS legal_business_name,
    uniqExact(fiscal_year) AS fiscal_years_listed,
    min(fiscal_year) AS first_directory_year,
    max(fiscal_year) AS latest_directory_year,
    uniqExactIf(piid, piid != '') AS distinct_piids
FROM sba_subcontracting_plans.contracts
WHERE unique_entity_id != ''
GROUP BY unique_entity_id
HAVING fiscal_years_listed > 1
ORDER BY fiscal_years_listed DESC, legal_business_name ASC
LIMIT 100

Agency and NAICS:
SELECT
    contracting_department_name,
    naics_code,
    uniqExactIf(unique_entity_id, unique_entity_id != '') AS distinct_primes,
    uniqExactIf(piid, piid != '') AS distinct_piids
FROM sba_subcontracting_plans.contracts
WHERE contracting_department_name != '' AND naics_code != ''
GROUP BY contracting_department_name, naics_code
ORDER BY distinct_primes DESC
LIMIT 100

Potentially ongoing records:
SELECT
    legal_business_name,
    unique_entity_id,
    piid,
    contracting_agency_name,
    naics_code,
    completion_date,
    last_date_to_order,
    source_url
FROM sba_subcontracting_plans.contracts
WHERE
    (completion_date IS NULL OR completion_date >= today())
    OR (last_date_to_order IS NOT NULL AND last_date_to_order >= today())
ORDER BY coalesce(last_date_to_order, completion_date) ASC
LIMIT 100
